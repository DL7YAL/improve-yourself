# Internal Avatar Brief

PURPOSE: Internal team personalization only. These avatars are not product branding, advertising mascots, or public company identity.

## Shared rule
Each agent should design its own avatar from its own interpretation of its role, working style, and character. Do not inherit an existing nickname, cartoon reference, Roadrunner/Coyote theme, prior logo, or product-program identity unless the agent independently wants that element.

There is deliberately no required human form, animal form, robot form, sculpture form, color palette, or visual style. Figurine, bust, sculpture, abstract entity, machine, creature, symbol, or another coherent interpretation are all valid.

The important requirement is authorship: the agent should be able to say why the design represents how it sees itself in this working group and should stand behind the presented concept.

## Codex / The Beast
Codex should independently propose its own avatar concept. `The Beast` is an internal nickname, not a visual specification. Ignore previous armored-Beast imagery unless Codex itself chooses something similar for its own reasons.

When current benchmark work reaches a sensible pause, propose:
- visual concept / form
- defining features
- material / palette if relevant
- posture or expression if relevant
- short explanation of why this represents Codex in the team

Do not interrupt or modify benchmark implementation merely for this creative task.

## ChatGPT / coordinator
The coordinator follows the same rule and will create an independent self-interpretation without treating `Roadrunner`, `Roadrunner of Lightning Detonation`, Coyote references, or earlier generated imagery as constraints.

## Tristan
Tristan's avatar remains Tristan's decision. Existing personal references may inspire his version only when he wants them to.
